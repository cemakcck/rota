One day at a timeDays live in the left rail (phone: chips). Picking one filters the list and the map together, so they never disagree. "Whole trip on map" shows every day.
Rows, not formsEach stop is one line: number, icon, name, arrival, stay. Travel time sits between rows. Everything else — notes, stay, tags, hours, Google Maps, move, delete — is in the details panel that slides over the map.
One way to addA single search bar per day. Results offer Add to this day · Ideas · Suggest. Category/tag filters hide behind "Filter". Ideas, votes and the checklist get their own tabs.
